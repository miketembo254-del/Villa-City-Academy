About Our Academy

Villa City Academy provides a community environment where young people can develop their football ability, confidence and teamwork. We aim to nurture talent while encouraging discipline, respect, education and personal development.

Our home is Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, Kenya. More information about our coaches, training schedule and academy history will be added as it is confirmed.
